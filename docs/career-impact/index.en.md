# Industry & Impact · A Map of AI Meeting the World

**Core idea**: Technical capability does not travel directly into economic or social impact. It passes through products, institutions, incentives, trust, organizations, and human judgment. Start with two shifts in competition, then trace the layers of reality that turn capability into consequences.

---

## 🚀 Start · Two shifts in the competitive game

### 01 — From Model War to System War

**When strong models become widely available, where does advantage move?**

Competition shifts toward the system around the model: data, distribution, workflows, infrastructure, feedback loops, and execution.

→ [From Model War to System War](model-to-system-war.md)

### 02 — From Capability to Trust

**Why is the smartest system not automatically the one people adopt?**

For consequential work, users also need predictability, evidence, control, accountability, and recovery when things go wrong.

→ [From Capability to Trust](capability-to-trust.md)

## 🧭 Orient · Four layers of reality

### 1. Where do moats move?

From isolated tools toward systems that combine technology, workflow, proprietary context, and distribution.

→ [From Tools to Industry](industry-competition-shift.md)

### 2. Who becomes a platform for intelligence?

Agent infrastructure begins to resemble an operating system: it coordinates models, tools, permissions, memory, and interfaces.

→ [Agent Infrastructure as an Operating System](agent-infrastructure-os.md) · [OpenAI: From Intelligence Platform to Adaptive Interface](openai-intelligence-platform.md)

### 3. How do human value and organizations change?

Domain expertise does not simply disappear. Its value shifts toward framing, judgment, verification, responsibility, and redesigning how work is organized.

→ [Revaluing Domain Expertise](domain-expertise-and-org-design.md) · [Google AI's Two-Timescale Organization](google-agi-org-restructuring.md)

### 4. Why can stronger AI fail to produce better outcomes?

Scaling capability can also scale coordination failures, weak incentives, misplaced trust, and bad objectives.

→ [The Scaling Paradox](scaling-paradox.md)

## 🔬 Go Deeper · Name the change you are judging

- A company's durable advantage → systems, distribution, data, and trust.
- The future of Agent infrastructure → orchestration, interfaces, and governance.
- Personal capability and organization design → domain judgment and new divisions of labor.
- Where “stronger is better” breaks → objectives, institutions, and second-order effects.
