# Belinda's Learning Wiki

A growing personal knowledge base about AI, computing, and the ideas around them.

## About This Wiki

This is not an encyclopedia or a pile of notes. Each chapter builds a natural chain of questions: understand the problem first, earn terminology when it becomes useful, then upgrade the mental model where the first approximation breaks.

## First Visit?

Begin with [Start Here](start-here.md). It builds the minimum map before sending you into detail.

## Three Ways In

- **Learn:** follow [Start Here](start-here.md) for a guided path.
- **Look Up:** use the [Glossary](glossary.md) or [Concept Index](index-all-concepts.md) when one term blocks you.
- **Explore:** read [Mental Models](mental-models.md) to see how the ideas changed over time.

## How to Read

You do not need to read everything in order. Begin with a territory map, follow the question that matters to you, and return to the glossary only when a term becomes necessary.

Conversation records preserve the original learning process in Chinese. Formal articles are the refined bilingual learning path.
